# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients (companies and independent contractors), recruiters evaluating dev profile, collaborators and fellow developers. They arrive with intent: looking to validate Victor's work, skill depth, and communication style before reaching out. Context is desktop and mobile, often a quick scan before a slower return visit.

## Product Purpose

Victor M. Santos personal portfolio. Shows who he is, what he builds, and how to reach him. Success means a visitor understands his level and personality in under 60 seconds and feels confident enough to contact him.

## Brand Personality

Precise, understated, craft-first. The portfolio reflects Victor's actual work ethic: no decoration for decoration's sake, everything earns its place. Tone is confident without being loud, personal without being informal.

## Anti-references

- Template-y portfolios that look like every Framer / Vercel starter
- Framer motion showcases that are 80% animation and 20% content
- Over-colorful dev portfolios with accent rainbows
- "Brutalist" portfolios that mistake illegibility for edge
- Portfolios that bury content under a splash animation

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint is a statement.** Monochromatic palette, measured motion. Every element added is a choice, not a reflex.
2. **Typography carries the personality.** Outfit for clarity, Instrument Serif italic for warmth and editorial contrast. Type is the identity system.
3. **Content before chrome.** Projects, writing, and contact info surface fast. No gatekeeping behind hero animations.
4. **Performant by default.** ISR, static generation, zero unnecessary client bundles. The portfolio itself is proof of craft.
5. **Confidence without noise.** No buzzwords, no superlatives. The work speaks; the copy supports it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion respected. Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements. Sufficient contrast at both light and dark themes.
